# Long-Term Vision

AthletIQ should become the central platform where:

* Athletes log and analyze training.
* Coaches monitor performance and recovery.
* Clubs manage teams and attendance.
* Organizations oversee athlete development.
* AI assists decision-making.
* Wearables provide real-time performance data.

The platform should evolve from a training log into a complete athletics performance ecosystem.
